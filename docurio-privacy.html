Privacy Policy for Docurio

Last updated: September 3, 2026

This policy explains what data Docurio ("the app") collects and how it's used. Docurio is a camera-to-PDF scanning app developed for Android.

Camera and scanned documents

Docurio uses your device's camera to capture photos of documents, which you crop, filter, and combine into PDF files entirely on your device. Scanned images and the PDFs built from them are stored locally in the app's private storage and are never uploaded to us or to any third party. We have no access to the content of your scans. Deleting a scan from the app permanently removes it from your device.

Permissions

Camera — used only while you're actively scanning a document. Not used in the background, and no video or audio is recorded.

Advertising

Docurio shows ads served by Google AdMob to support free use of the app. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), general location (derived from IP address), and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads, and may use this data to personalize ads unless you've limited ad tracking in your device settings. See Google's Privacy & Terms for details on how Google handles this data.

Analytics and crash reporting

Docurio uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics (Google) to understand app usage and diagnose crashes. This includes device information, app events (e.g., screens viewed, features used), and crash logs. This data is tied to an anonymous installation identifier, not your name or account — Docurio does not require or collect an account, name, or email address. See Firebase's Privacy Policy for details.

Data we do not collect

Docurio does not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, location (beyond the coarse, IP-based location AdMob may use for ad delivery), or any content from your scanned documents.

Data sharing

We do not sell your data. The only data shared with third parties is what's described above under Advertising and Analytics, shared with Google (AdMob/Firebase) to provide those services.

Children's privacy

Docurio is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
