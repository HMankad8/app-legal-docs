Privacy Policy for Nubero

Last updated: September 3, 2026

This policy explains what data Nubero ("the app") collects and how it's used. Nubero is an everyday calculator suite (BMI, age, date difference, and more) developed for Android.

Calculations and history

Every calculation you run (BMI, age, date difference, etc.) is performed entirely on your device. Nubero keeps a local calculation history so you can revisit past results — this history is stored only in the app's private storage on your device and is never uploaded to us or to any third party. Clearing an entry from history permanently removes it from your device.

Permissions

Nubero requests no sensitive device permissions — it does not access your camera, contacts, location, or files. It uses internet access solely to request ads and send analytics/crash data, described below.

Advertising

Nubero shows ads served by Google AdMob to support free use of the app. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), general location (derived from IP address), and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads, and may use this data to personalize ads unless you've limited ad tracking in your device settings. See Google's Privacy & Terms for details on how Google handles this data.

Analytics and crash reporting

Nubero uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics (Google) to understand app usage and diagnose crashes. This includes device information, app events (e.g., which calculators are used), and crash logs. This data is tied to an anonymous installation identifier, not your name or account — Nubero does not require or collect an account, name, or email address. See Firebase's Privacy Policy for details.

Data we do not collect

Nubero does not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, precise location, or the content of any calculation beyond what's stored locally in your own history.

Data sharing

We do not sell your data. The only data shared with third parties is what's described above under Advertising and Analytics, shared with Google (AdMob/Firebase) to provide those services.

Children's privacy

Nubero is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
